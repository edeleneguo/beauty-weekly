Fragrance Industry News Section 01

GLOBALEstée Lauder FY26 Fragrance Hits $2.779B; Le Labo China +50%Market▾
FY2026 fragrance revenue $2.779bn, +12% YoY — the group's fastest-growing category; mainland China fragrance grew double digits, with Le Labo up more than 50%.
Estée Lauder's FY2026 results put fragrance at $2.779bn, up 12% year on year and ahead of every other category in the group. Management told the earnings call that mainland China fragrance delivered double-digit growth and that Le Labo sales rose more than 50%, making the niche house the clearest beneficiary of China's premiumisation of scent. For NPD, the read-through is that prestige niche — not mass — is where Chinese fragrance demand is compounding.
GLOBALPuig H1 2026: Fragrance & Fashion Reach 73% of Group RevenueMarket▾
Puig H1 2026 net revenue €2.354bn (+4.4%); fragrance and fashion = 73% of the total; Asia-Pacific +20.9%. L'Oréal H1 fragrance also grew double digits.
Puig reported H1 2026 net revenue of €2.354bn, up 4.4%, with its fragrance and fashion division accounting for 73% of group revenue and Asia-Pacific growing 20.9% — the fastest region. Carolina Herrera, Byredo and Dries Van Noten all delivered double-digit growth. L'Oréal separately reported H1 2026 sales of €23.776bn (+6.8%), with its luxury division at €7.997bn (+4.4%) and fragrance growing double digits across Prada, Armani, YSL and Valentino. Across three majors, fragrance is the only category consistently outgrowing the portfolio.
USTresalis Takes Majority Stake in Vyrao as Fragrance M&A ReopensCapital▾
Tresalis acquires a majority stake in wellness-adjacent fragrance house Vyrao; founder Yasmin Sewell stays on as Chief Brand Officer.
Tresalis has taken a majority stake in Vyrao, the fragrance house built around wellness positioning and energy-clearing storytelling. Founder Yasmin Sewell remains as Chief Brand Officer, keeping creative control with the founder while the new owner funds scale. The deal is one of the earliest consolidating moves in the wellness-adjacent fragrance segment, and follows the same pattern as recent beauty deals: buy the brand equity, keep the founder in the building.
USScent Beauty Names Former L'Oréal Executive Yoana Land as CFOEvent▾
Scent Beauty appoints ex-L'Oréal finance executive Yoana Land as CFO, adding group-scale financial discipline to a licensing-led fragrance player.
Scent Beauty, the licensing-driven fragrance company behind celebrity and designer scent portfolios, has appointed former L'Oréal executive Yoana Land as Chief Financial Officer. The hire signals a shift from launch-velocity growth to margin and portfolio discipline: licensed fragrance businesses carry high royalty loads, and financial control is usually the lever pulled before a scale-up or a sale. Land is one of several ex-L'Oréal operators now sitting in US fragrance C-suites.
CNChinese Beauty Majors File Into Fragrance: 9 SKUs from Roopy, 3 from KansMarket▾
Domestic beauty groups filed a wave of fragrance SKUs with the NMPA — Roopy (9), Kans (3), and Original Color Pagoda's first scent — all still pre-launch.
Checks of National Medical Products Administration filings show a fresh wave of domestic fragrance registrations. Hand-cream brand Roopy filed nine "绮境" (Dreamland) eau de parfums at once — 75ml full size through 8ml minis and sample boxes — including 融雪之时, a scent already used in its hand cream, body lotion and solid perfume. Kans filed three 红运 fragrances on 3 September (filling: 惠州兆婷), and Proya's Original Color Pagoda filed its first fragrance, so far only as a 4ml sample (filling: 中山中研). None have gone on sale yet. The pattern is consistent: port a proven scent into a new format rather than launch an unknown one.

Scoring Methodology

Heat scores do not pursue absolute heat — they serve relative ranking among peer products. Core principle: only signals from the specific product SKU itself may be counted; brand heat must never substitute for product heat.

Total Score Structure

Heat Score = Sales Score (≤50) + Buzz Score (≤50), max 100.
Adjustable by category: Skincare/Efficacy 65/35 · Makeup 55/45 · Fragrance/New Makeup 45/55 · Luxury/Niche 40/60.
MVP stage uses 50/50.

Sales Score Formula

Sales Signal = Base × Platform Authority × Evidence Confidence × Time Decay
Countable signals: Bestseller / ranking / monthly sales·GMV / Sold Out·Restock / Hot·Trending tags / multi-channel distribution / authoritative retail endorsement
Evidence Confidence: explicit screenshot ×1.0 · platform official tag ×0.8 · third-party article mention ×0.6 · manual judgment ×0.4

Buzz Score Formula

Buzz Signal = Base × Platform Authority × Buzz Type Weight × Time Decay
Buzz three-tier weight: Organic ×1.0 > Editorial ×0.75 > Paid/Seeded ×0.45
Organic: user posts/comments/UGC/Reddit · Editorial: Allure/Vogue/NYMag/Cosmopolitan/Byrdie/WhoWhatWear · Paid: brand BGC/PR seeding/creator collab

Time Decay

0-30d ×1.0 · 31-90d ×0.8 · 91-180d ×0.6 · 181-365d ×0.4 · >1yr ×0.2
Long-term bestsellers may retain higher weight with continued evidence.

Display Score Tiers

90-98 Viral · 85-89 Trending · 80-84 Strong Interest · 75-79 Rising · 70-74 Niche Signal · 65-69 Low Confidence
Internal raw_score drives true ranking, never tampered; display_score capped at 98, floored at 65.

Core rule: A product cannot score high simply because its brand is hot. Only SKU-level sales/ranking/editorial/user-discussion/social/restock evidence counts. Brand heat without product-level evidence → background info only, no heat added.

Cross-panel consistency: Heat rankings and new-product radar use the same scoring formula and reporting-cycle data. The same product must have identical scores across both panels. The two panels differ only in product selection scope (Heat = market-wide TOP10 / Radar = last-4-week new launches); calculation is identical.
